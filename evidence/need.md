# Evidence of a local information need

These primary documents support the problem and the intended use of public data. They do not establish Fieldwork adoption, a measured benefit, or a causal relationship between neighborhood records and health. The older dataset descriptions remain separate evidence of availability.

## 1. CDC: About PLACES: Local Data for Better Health

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/places/about/index.html

Accessed: 2026-10-24 (UTC). Opened from the VPS; quotation checked against the retrieved document.

> Local health problems and priority health risk behaviors for action.

CDC identifies local health problems and priorities as a use of PLACES. This does not establish use of Fieldwork.

## 2. CDC: Frequently Asked Questions — Using the Data

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/places/faqs/index.html

Accessed: 2026-10-24 (UTC). Opened from the VPS; quotation checked against the retrieved document.
